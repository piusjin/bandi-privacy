Bandi

Business cards to contacts

Photograph the business cards you collect, one at a time or several in one shot. Bandi reads each card for you, and you decide with a swipe which people go into your phone's contacts and which stay in Bandi.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

How it works

  1. Scan. Photograph one card, or lay out a few and take one photo.
  2. Keep going. Cards are read in the background, and Bandi tells you when they're ready.
  3. Sort. Swipe right to add someone to your phone's contacts, left to keep them only in Bandi, or check off a whole batch at once.

What it does

  • Several cards, one photoBandi finds each card in the photo and splits them apart.
  • No duplicatesAlready in your contacts? Bandi spots the match instead of adding them twice.
  • Remember where you metFile cards under an event, straight from your calendar.
  • On every phone you useYour cards are kept in your Bandi account, so a new phone doesn't lose them.
  • Yours to takeExport everything to a spreadsheet (CSV) whenever you like.
  • Your choice on AICards are read by an AI service only if you allow it. Otherwise you type them in.

Privacy

No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold. You can delete any card, or your whole account and everything in it, from inside the app. The full details are in the privacy policy.
