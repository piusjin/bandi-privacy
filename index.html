Privacy Policy

Bandi · Effective Date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bandi ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use the App on iPhone or Android. Bandi turns photos of business cards into contacts. The App is developed and operated by an individual developer, not a company. If you have questions about this policy, contact sg.piuskim@gmail.com.

In short: your cards are kept in your Bandi account so they survive a new phone. Card photos are read by a third-party AI service to fill in the details. We don't show ads, don't track you, and don't sell or share your data for marketing. You can export everything, or delete your account and all of its data, from inside the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Your account

Bandi requires an account. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name (if you share it), your email address, and an identifier for your account from that service. We never see your Apple or Google password. If you use Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email," we receive only the relay address Apple gives us.

1.2 Business cards you scan

When you scan a card, we store in your account:

  • the photo of the card (or of several cards taken together);
  • the details read from it: typically a person's name, job title, company, phone numbers, email, address and website;
  • what you add: notes, where you met, the event you filed the card under, and whether you saved the card to your phone's contacts.

These cards describe other people. Please scan only cards you were given, and use the details as their owner would expect.

1.3 My Info

If you fill in My Info (your own name, title, company, email and phone, shown as a QR code you can share), we store it in your account.

1.4 Information that stays on your phone

  • Contacts: with your permission, Bandi adds cards to your phone's contacts and checks your existing contacts so it doesn't add someone twice. Your address book is read on the phone and is not uploaded. To keep track across your phones, we store only which contact a card was saved to: an identifier for that entry, not its contents.
  • Calendar: with your permission, Bandi lists a day's events so you can file cards under one. Your calendar is read on the phone only. Just the name of the event you choose is saved on the cards.
  • Camera: used only to photograph cards.
  • Notifications: created on the phone to tell you when cards are ready. We don't use push notification services.
  • Exports: spreadsheets you export are saved on your phone, and go only where you send them.
  • Debug log: a technical log kept on the phone. It leaves the phone only if you choose to share it with us.

1.5 Technical information

Like any online service, our servers keep standard technical logs, such as the time of a request, your IP address and error details, for security and troubleshooting. We count how many card photos you submit each day to apply the daily limit (100 photos a day). The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

2. How We Use Information

  • To read your card photos and fill in the details.
  • To keep your cards in your account and in sync across your phones.
  • To add cards to your phone's contacts when you ask it to.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To apply the daily reading limit, and to fix problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we do not sell it.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Names, email addresses and phone numbers are additionally encrypted in our database with keys held in Google Cloud's key management service.
  • Card photos are stored in Google Cloud Storage, encrypted at rest, and reachable only through your account.
  • Your sign-in tokens are kept in the phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain, or Android's protected app storage).

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data Retention

  • Your cards and photos are kept for as long as you have an account.
  • A card you delete is removed from the App straight away and permanently erased from our servers, photo included, after 30 days. The delay lets your other phones catch up with the deletion.
  • When you delete your account, your account, cards, photos and My Info are deleted from our servers immediately. Copies in our providers' short-term backups expire within days.
  • Technical server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • See and correct: every card can be viewed and edited in the App.
  • Export: export all your cards to a spreadsheet (CSV) at any time.
  • Delete: delete any card, or your whole account, from inside the App (Settings → Delete account). Deleting an account created with Sign in with Apple also revokes Bandi's access to your Apple ID.
  • Permissions: you can turn off camera, contacts, calendar or notification access at any time in your phone's settings. The App keeps working with what you allow.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. Contact us to exercise them; we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Bandi is a tool for business networking and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Users

Our servers are in South Korea, and our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. By using Bandi, you understand that your information is transferred to and processed in these countries, with the protections described in this policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before a change applies to you.
